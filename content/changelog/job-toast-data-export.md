Added a download in Settings that collects a person's Job Toast records into one JSON file. It includes their jobs, notes, preparation and other saved account data, with links to resume files.

The export leaves out passwords, tokens and internal processing queues. People can retrieve their records directly instead of emailing me for a copy.
