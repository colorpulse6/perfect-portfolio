Rebuilt the calendar around the events of a job search: interviews, calls, preparation, networking and deadlines. Events can carry a job link, meeting details, notes and an in-app reminder.

Month, week and day views share the same event cards. Adding an interview against a job fills its next available interview slot, so the calendar and application record stay together.
