Released the Cerebro public beta: mission control for AI agents, as a native Mac app. A Conductor plans the work, delegates to Claude and Codex agents, follows their handoffs, and asks before it crosses a line you set. Agents, workflows, and terminals share one surface.

Version 0.1.0 is signed and notarized, runs on macOS 14 or newer on Apple silicon, and works with the Claude Code or Codex CLI you already have.
