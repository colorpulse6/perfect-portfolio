Rebuilt the Atlas as a WebGL galaxy map around a black hole with real gravitational lensing. Every project, album, and piece of writing is a named star you can hover, click, or zoom toward, and clicking a star's title opens the same details.

The home page now reaches it through a wormhole: the page bends into a lensed tunnel and opens onto the Atlas. Browsers without WebGL2 still get the classic Atlas.
