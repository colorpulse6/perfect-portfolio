Havenwood now reaches beyond the courtyard into the seaside lane, harbour, church square and hill village. The public record has new in-game views of those places, the coast path and sea walls, alongside the save system that remembers where Kai stood.

The townsfolk pages also put their drawings beside their game figures and dialogue. This is another step in turning the painted town into a connected place to explore.
