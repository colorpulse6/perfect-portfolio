Turned the colony's list of destinations into a spatial region map. Landmarks sit on their saved coordinates over a contour backdrop, with the expedition's origin marked and the selected route highlighted.

A compact card gives the next survey, travel or founding action. The map keeps the same discovery rules and costs, with layouts for desktop and phone.
