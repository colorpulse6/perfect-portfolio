Brought Sector Zero's persistent galaxy, colony development and first-person missions closer together. Ashfall gained a five-building foundation, visible completed buildings and guidance for power and mining.

Ground missions gained the new grunt, charger and sentry figures, a separate Interact action, and fixes for disconnected objectives and blocked routes. The companion site now follows that same direction: travel, build a settlement, and take on work on the ground.
