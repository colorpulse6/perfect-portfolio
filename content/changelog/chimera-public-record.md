Rebuilt Chimera's site around the painted Unreal game now taking shape. It brings together the world, characters, story, visual direction, dated build notes and the first gameplay recording.

The site has some of the game's unease: paint that slips into ink, a hum, and a record that sometimes corrects itself. It is a place to follow development; the game is still in progress.
