Started turning Sector Zero into one continuous galaxy with consequences. Faction standing now changes prices, greetings, and whether a trader will deal with you at all, and the simulation runs on a fixed timestep.

The galaxy map is becoming the game's source of truth: saves persist, routes and travel have real costs, and encounters launch from where you actually are. This part is still in active development.
