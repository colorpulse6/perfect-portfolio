Added a way out of empty stocks on Ashfall. A Salvage Shift advances the world and earns metal and travel supplies without an entry cost or a fight, giving a stalled colony a route back into building and exploration.

The fighter mode also got a visual pass: clearer shadows, corrected enemy proportions, and improved shots, exhaust, explosions and bosses. Its combat rules and rewards stay the same.
