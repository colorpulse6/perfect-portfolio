Added date grouping to the Job Toast board, so recent saves sit together under Today, Yesterday, This Week and older groups. Sorting now survives a reload.

The extension also gained a Mark as Applied button and a save flow that carries the posting into the side panel when it opens. These changes keep finding a job and recording the application closer together.
